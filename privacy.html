Privacy policy

Shorts Pipeline · Last updated 11 October 2026

Who uses this app

Shorts Pipeline is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app accesses:

  • the ability to upload, schedule and update videos on the owner's YouTube channels (YouTube Data API);
  • performance reports for those channels, such as views, watch time, subscribers gained and estimated revenue (YouTube Analytics API).

It does not access any other Google data, and it does not access data belonging to anyone other than the owner.

How the data is used and stored

Channel statistics are used only to show the owner a private performance dashboard and to decide which video topics to make next. Sign-in tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Shorts Pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored statistics are deleted within 30 days of revocation.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us through the email listed on any of our YouTube channels (Money Bits, Mind Upgrade, Clip Desk).
